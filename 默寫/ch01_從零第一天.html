Part 1 · cube geometry (the whole packing chapter lives here)

M1 — Face diagonal

A cube has edge $a$. Draw a square face. Using Pythagoras, find the length of the face diagonal.

邊長 $a$ 的立方體，面對角線多長？畢氏定理。
Paper — draw the square
$a^2+a^2=d^2$ → $d=\sqrt2\,a$. This is the contact line in fcc.

M2 — Body diagonal

Same cube. Find the body diagonal (corner to opposite corner through the interior).

體對角線（穿過內部、角到對角）多長？
Paper
Face diagonal $\sqrt2\,a$, then $d^2=(\sqrt2\,a)^2+a^2=3a^2$ → $d=\sqrt3\,a$. Contact line in bcc.

M3 — Counting shared atoms

A cube has 8 corners. Each corner atom is shared by 8 cubes. How many corner atoms belong to this cube? A cube has 6 faces; each face-centre atom is shared by 2 cubes. How many face-centre atoms belong to this cube?

8 個角、各被 8 個立方共用 → 這個立方分到幾顆？6 個面心、各被 2 個共用 → 幾顆？
Paper
Corners: $8\times\tfrac18=1$. Faces: $6\times\tfrac12=3$. Together with no body-centre → fcc has $N=4$. sc is corners only, $N=1$. bcc is corners + 1 body centre, $N=2$.

M4 — Sphere volume

Write the volume of a sphere of radius $r$. Then substitute $r=a/2$ and simplify $N\cdot\frac43\pi r^3/a^3$ for $N=1$.

球體積公式。代入 $r=a/2$、$N=1$，化簡填充率。
Paper
$V=\frac43\pi r^3$. $r=a/2$ → $\eta=\frac43\pi(a/2)^3/a^3=\pi/6$. That is sc packing.

M5 — Reciprocals (Miller engine)

A plane cuts the axes at $4$, $1$, and $2$ (in units of $a_1,a_2,a_3$). Take reciprocals, then clear fractions to the smallest integers. Write $(hkl)$.

截距 4, 1, 2 → 取倒數 → 化最小整數 → Miller 指標。
Paper
Reciprocals $\frac14,1,\frac12$. Multiply by 4 → $(1,4,2)$ i.e. $(142)$.

M6 — Vector length

$\mathbf v=\frac a2(\hat x+\hat y)$. Find $|\mathbf v|$.

這就是 fcc 一條原胞向量的長度。
Paper
$|\mathbf v|=\frac a2\sqrt{1+1}=\frac a2\sqrt2$. (fcc primitive vector length.)
