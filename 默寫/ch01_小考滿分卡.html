Q1 · Define lattice, basis, crystal structure. Write $\mathbf T$.

定義三個詞，寫平移向量。

Lattice: the set of mathematical points $\mathbf R=n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3$ with $n_i$ integers.
Basis: the atom or group of atoms attached to each lattice point.
Crystal structure = lattice $\times$ basis.

$\mathbf T=n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3,\quad n_i\in\mathbb Z$

One sentence for the paper: A crystal structure is a lattice of points together with a basis of atoms on each point.

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1, lattice translation vectors and “basis and the crystal structure”.

Q2 · fcc: $N$, CN, contact, derive $\eta$.

fcc：每胞原子數、配位數、相切、推出填充率。不要只寫 0.74。
  1. $N=8\times\frac18+6\times\frac12=4$ atoms per conventional cube.
  2. CN $=12$ (nearest neighbours along face diagonals).
  3. Contact along the face diagonal: four radii on $\sqrt2\,a$, so $4r=\sqrt2\,a$, $r=\sqrt2\,a/4$.
  4. $\eta=\dfrac{4\cdot\frac43\pi r^3}{a^3}$. Substitute $r^3=(\sqrt2)^3 a^3/64=2\sqrt2\,a^3/64=\sqrt2\,a^3/32$:
    $\eta=4\cdot\frac43\pi\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}{32}=\dfrac{\pi\sqrt2}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3\sqrt2}\approx0.740$.
fcc: $N=4$, CN $=12$, $4r=\sqrt2\,a$, $\eta=\pi\sqrt2/6=\pi/(3\sqrt2)\approx0.74$

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1 Table 2 (points per cell, CN, nn $=a/\sqrt2$, packing $\pi\sqrt2/6$) and Fig. 8.

Q5 · CsCl: Bravais lattice? Basis? Why not bcc?

角是 Cs、體心是 Cl。看起來像 bcc，但不是。

Cs at the 8 corners, Cl at the body centre (or swapped).

  1. Corner and centre are different chemical species → they are not equivalent lattice points.
  2. A bcc lattice requires every point (corner and centre) to be the same environment. Here they are not.
  3. Therefore the Bravais lattice is simple cubic, with a two-atom basis {Cs, Cl}.
  4. Count: $8\times\frac18=1$ Cs and $1$ Cl per cube. Ratio $1:1$.
CsCl = sc lattice + 2-atom basis. Not bcc.

Exam sentence: CsCl is not bcc because the corner and body-centre sites are occupied by different atoms, so they are not equivalent lattice points.

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1 Fig. 18: space lattice simple cubic; basis Cs⁺ at 000, Cl⁻ at $\tfrac12\tfrac12\tfrac12$.
