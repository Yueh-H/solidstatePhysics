What the English usually does: define a phrase in parentheses (“filling factor”), ask you to draw, identify, calculate, or explain why not. Full marks = English sentence + one derivation line + a sketch if it says draw.
英文題習慣：括號裡給定義、要求 draw / identify / calculate / explain why not。滿分＝一句英文＋一行推導＋題目說 draw 就要畫。

A · Real qualifying-exam English (crystal-structure block)

QE 2020 · packing · real wording

Calculate the maximum portion of the volume that spherical atoms can fill (i.e. the filling factor) when arranged in the following three cubic lattices.
這題在考什麼。 filling factor = packing fraction $\eta$。三個立方＝sc / bcc / fcc（原卷有圖）。不要只寫 0.52／0.68／0.74，要有 $2r$ 或 $4r$。
老師用詞。 他可能寫 filling factor、packing fraction、atomic packing factor，都是 $\eta$。
sc $\pi/6$；bcc $\pi\sqrt3/8$；fcc $\pi\sqrt2/6$. Show contact: $2r=a$, $4r=\sqrt3 a$, $4r=\sqrt2 a$. Kittel Table 2.

QE 2020 · diamond hardness · real wording

Diamond exhibits the highest hardness of any material. Can this be explained by the packing density of its crystal structure?
這題在考什麼。 答案是 No。鑽石 $\eta\approx0.34$、CN＝4，比 fcc 空。硬度來自四面體共價鍵，不是 packing。
怎麼寫。 先一句 No，再給 $\eta$ 與 CN，再講 covalent bonds。
No. Diamond packing $\approx0.34$ (emptier than sc). CN $=4$. Hardness from directional $sp^3$ bonds, not density. Kittel § Diamond.

QE 2024 · Bravais from vectors · real wording

Someone tells you he found a crystal with the following lattice vectors: [three vectors in $\hat x,\hat y,\hat z$].
(1) Identify the Bravais lattice (drawing a picture may help).
(2) Calculate the volume of the primitive cell.
這題在考什麼。 給 $\mathbf a_1,\mathbf a_2,\mathbf a_3$，認 sc／bcc／fcc。畫圖加分。體積 $V=|\mathbf a_1\cdot(\mathbf a_2\times\mathbf a_3)|$。
fcc 指紋。 三支等長、夾角 $60^\circ$，連到面心。bcc 連到體心。
If they are the three face-centre vectors $\frac a2(\hat x+\hat y)$ etc. → fcc, $V=a^3/4$. If body-centre type → bcc, $V=a^3/2$. Kittel Fig. 10–11.

QE 2024 · hcp · real wording

Consider the hcp structure that is produced by stacking layers of equal-size spheres.
(1) What is the ratio between unit cell height and width?
(2) What is the atomic packing factor?
這題在考什麼。 height/width = $c/a=\sqrt{8/3}\approx1.633$。APF 與 fcc 相同 $0.74$。
用詞。 ratio between height and width = $c/a$。atomic packing factor = $\eta$。
(1) $\sqrt{8/3}\approx1.633$. (2) $\pi\sqrt2/6\approx0.740$ (same as fcc). Kittel Fig. 19–21.

QE 2019 · lattice vs basis · real wording

Draw the lattice for a crystal with the three-atom basis $A=(0,1)$, $B=(1,1)$, $C=(0,0)$ and the lattice vectors $\mathbf r=(2,1)$ and $\mathbf s=(1,3)$.
這題在考什麼。 先畫格點（只靠 $\mathbf r,\mathbf s$ 的整數組合），再在每個格點掛 A,B,C 三原子。很多人只畫一組 ABC 就停。
小考短版。 Define lattice and basis. Give one example (NaCl or CsCl).
Dots at $n\mathbf r+m\mathbf s$. At every dot, copy the three atoms A,B,C. Crystal = lattice × basis. Kittel § basis.

QE 2019 · what is a crystal · real wording

What properties make a crystal different from a liquid? (5%)
這題在考什麼。 長程平移週期：存在 $\mathbf T=n_i\mathbf a_i$。液體近程有序、沒有格子。
5% 短答。 兩句英文就夠，不要寫作文。
A crystal has long-range translational order: a discrete lattice of points and a repeating basis. A liquid has only short-range order, no $\mathbf T$ that maps it onto itself at arbitrary distance.

B · Reconstructed 8-minute in-class quiz (same English)

These are not leaked quizzes. They use the same verbs: calculate / identify / draw / explain. One question, 8–12 minutes, last lecture.

Pop quiz · likely

Define the terms lattice and basis. Write the general lattice translation $\mathbf T$.
中文在問。 晶格是什麼、基元是什麼、平移向量公式。
滿分形。 Lattice = mathematical points. Basis = atoms on each point. Crystal = lattice × basis. Then the $\mathbf T$ formula. 不要只寫中文「格子」「原子」。
Lattice: set of mathematical points. Basis: atom(s) on each point. $\mathbf T=n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3$.

Pop quiz · likely

For the face-centered cubic conventional cell, give the number of lattice points, the coordination number, the hard-sphere contact condition, and the packing fraction (derive $\eta$, do not only quote 0.74).
中文在問。 fcc 慣用立方：每胞幾點、配位數、相切、$\\eta$ 要推。
用詞。 face-centered cubic 就是 fcc；conventional cell 就是你畫的那個立方。derive = 要有 $4r=\sqrt2 a$。
$N=4$, CN $=12$, $4r=\sqrt2 a$, $\eta=\pi\sqrt2/6$. Sketch: 8 corners + 6 face centres + red face diagonal.

Pop quiz · trap

CsCl has one ion at the cube corners and the other at the body centre. Identify the Bravais lattice and the basis. Why is this structure not body-centered cubic?
中文在問。 認 Bravais、寫 basis、為什麼不是 bcc。
關鍵英文。 “not equivalent lattice points” / “different chemical species”。Identify the Bravais lattice → 答 simple cubic。
Bravais: simple cubic. Basis: Cs + Cl. Not bcc because corner and centre are different ions, hence inequivalent. Kittel Fig. 18.

Pop quiz · Miller

A plane intercepts the crystal axes at $3a_1$, $2a_2$ and $2a_3$. Determine the Miller indices. For a cubic crystal with lattice constant $a$, write $d_{hkl}$.
中文在問。 截距 → Miller；再寫立方面間距公式。
用詞。 intercepts、Miller indices、lattice constant。$3,2,2\to(233)$，不是 (322)。
Reciprocals $\frac13,\frac12,\frac12$ → $(233)$. $d_{hkl}=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$.

Pop quiz · names

Write the full names of sc, bcc and fcc. In one sentence each, state where the extra lattice point(s) sit (if any).
中文在問。 三個全名＋多出來的格點在哪。
必寫英文。 simple cubic / body-centered cubic / face-centered cubic。body = interior；face = each square face。
sc simple cubic: corners only. bcc body-centered cubic: extra point at the body centre. fcc face-centered cubic: extra point at each face centre.
