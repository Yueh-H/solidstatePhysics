Names — write these in English on the quiz

Kittel Table 2 calls them simple, body-centered, and face-centered cubic lattices. The abbreviations sc / bcc / fcc are what you write on the paper. “Centered” means an extra lattice point sits at the centre of something — the cube’s interior, or each face.

Kittel 表 1.2 用 Simple / Body-centered / Face-centered。小考請寫英文全名或縮寫。centered＝多了一個格點，放在「體心」或「每個面的中心」。
Abbrev.Full name (English)中文What “centered” means
sc simple cubic
(also primitive cubic)		 簡單立方 Nothing extra: atoms only at the 8 corners. The conventional cube is the primitive cell.
bcc body-centered cubic 體心立方 Body = the interior of the cube. One extra identical atom at $\tfrac12\tfrac12\tfrac12$. Corners $+$ centre $=2$ lattice points.
fcc face-centered cubic 面心立方 Face = each of the 6 square faces. Extra identical atoms at the 6 face centres. Corners $+$ faces $=4$ lattice points.

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1, Fig. 8 (“The cubic space lattices”) and Table 2 (“Simple / Body-centered / Face-centered”). Fig. 8’s cells are conventional; only sc is primitive (Kittel: “of these only the sc is a primitive cell”).

One-line memory:

$$ \begin{aligned} \mathrm{sc}&=\text{simple cubic}=\text{corners only},\\ \mathrm{bcc}&=\text{body-centered cubic}=\text{corners}+\text{one in the body},\\ \mathrm{fcc}&=\text{face-centered cubic}=\text{corners}+\text{one on every face}. \end{aligned} $$
sc＝簡單立方：只有角，沒有「心」。
bcc＝體心立方：body＝立方體內部，正中心多 1 顆（和角上同種）。
fcc＝面心立方：face＝6 個正方形面，每個面正中多 1 顆。
CsCl 看起來像 bcc，但角與中心是不同元素，所以全名仍是 simple cubic + basis，不是 body-centered cubic（Kittel Fig. 18）。

0 · Build the cube (do this first, every time)

  1. Draw a square — this is the front face. Side $= a$.
  2. From each corner, draw a short line up and right (same length, about $\frac13 a$, same angle).
  3. Connect those four new points — back square.
  4. Dashed the three edges you cannot see (left-back, bottom-back, vertical-back if hidden).
①正面正方形 ②每角往右上畫一小段 ③連成後面的正方形 ④看不見的邊改虛線。

1 · sc — simple cubic — 8 blue corners only

  1. Draw the cube.
  2. Put a dot on all 8 corners. Stop. No extra dots.
  3. Mark one front edge in red — that is the contact: $$2r=a$$
  4. Write next to it: $$N=8\times\frac{1}{8}=1,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=6$$
只畫 8 個角。紅線畫一條正面的邊＝相切。$N=1$。
sc: 8 corners only. Red line = an edge. $$2r=a,\qquad N=1,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=6,\qquad \eta=\frac{\pi}{6}$$

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1 Fig. 8 (conventional sc cell); Table 2: lattice points per cell $=1$, nearest neighbours $=6$, nn distance $=a$, packing fraction $\pi/6=0.524$. Because nn $=a=2r$ for hard spheres in contact.

2 · bcc — body-centered cubic — 8 corners + 1 red centre

  1. Draw sc first (8 corners).
  2. Put one red dot in the middle of the cube (crossing of the body diagonals). Approximate: centre of the front square, then nudge up-right half the offset.
  3. Draw a red dashed body diagonal: front-bottom-left corner → back-top-right corner. Write on that line: $$4r=\sqrt{3}\,a$$
  4. Write: $$N=2,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=8$$
先畫 sc，再在立方正中心加一顆紅點。紅虛線＝體對角線（前下左 → 後上右），上面排 4 個半徑。
bcc: sc + 1 red centre. Red line = body diagonal. $$4r=\sqrt{3}\,a,\qquad N=2,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=8,\qquad \eta=\frac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{8}$$

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1 Fig. 8 (conventional bcc); Table 2: 2 lattice points per conventional cell, CN $=8$, nn distance $=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a$. Hard-sphere contact: $2r=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a$ ⇒ $4r=\sqrt3\,a$. Packing $\pi\sqrt3/8=0.680$. Primitive vectors: Fig. 10.

3 · fcc — face-centered cubic — 8 corners + 6 red face centres

  1. Draw sc (8 corners).
  2. On each of the 6 faces, put a red dot at the face centre (midpoint of the face). Visible faces: front, top, right. Hidden: back, bottom, left — still draw them (smaller / dashed if you want).
  3. On the front face, draw a red face diagonal (corner–face centre–corner). Write: $$4r=\sqrt{2}\,a$$
  4. Write: $$N=4,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=12$$
8 個角 + 每個面正中一顆紅點（6 面）。相切在面對角線：角—面心—角，4 個半徑。
fcc: 8 corners + 6 face centres. Red line = face diagonal. $$4r=\sqrt{2}\,a,\qquad N=4,\qquad \mathrm{CN}=12,\qquad \eta=\frac{\pi\sqrt{2}}{6}$$

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1 Fig. 8 (conventional fcc); Table 2: 4 lattice points per cell, CN $=12$, nn distance $=a/\sqrt2$. Hard-sphere contact: $2r=a/\sqrt2$ ⇒ $4r=\sqrt2\,a$. Packing $\pi\sqrt2/6=0.740$ (same as $\pi/(3\sqrt2)$). Primitive vectors to face centres: Fig. 11, angle $60^\circ$.

4 · Why the 4r (flat cut — this is the packing picture)

If the 3D cube is messy, draw this 2D cut next to it. Circles are to scale and actually touch on the red line.

立體畫亂了，旁邊畫 2D 切開。圓的半徑按公式，紅線上真的相切。bcc 不是正方形（正方形對角線是 $\sqrt2 a$），是 $a\times a\sqrt2$ 的矩形，對角線才是 $\sqrt3 a$。

Ref. Numbers: Kittel Table 2 nn distances. sc and fcc 2D panels are cube faces (Fig. 8). The bcc panel is not a Kittel figure — it is the Euclidean section of the cube by the plane $y=x$, chosen so the rectangle diagonal equals Table 2’s $\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a\times 2$. Do not draw bcc contact on a square (square diagonal $=\sqrt2 a$, that is fcc).

5 · CsCl — looks like bcc, draw it as sc + two colours

  1. Draw the same cube as bcc.
  2. Colour corners one species (Cs), centre the other (Cl). Write the two names.
  3. Caption: sc + 2-atom basis, NOT bcc — because the two sites are different atoms.
圖跟 bcc 一樣，但角與中心用兩種顏色／兩個元素名。圖下寫 NOT bcc。
Same drawing as bcc, two colours. Caption: not bcc.

Ref. Kittel 8e Ch. 1, § Cesium chloride structure, Fig. 18: “The space lattice is simple cubic, and the basis has one Cs⁺ ion at 000 and one Cl⁻ ion at $\tfrac12\tfrac12\tfrac12$.” CN $=8$ (unlike bcc’s 8 identical neighbours). Ashcroft & Mermin Ch. 4 / Ch. 7: a Bravais lattice has every point equivalent — two species ⇒ not a 1-atom bcc lattice.
