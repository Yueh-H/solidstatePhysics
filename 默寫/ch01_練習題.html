Set A · required · 40 min

A1 · draw Three cubic cells

Sketch the conventional cubes of sc, bcc, and fcc. For each, write: $N$ (atoms per conventional cell), coordination number CN, and the contact direction ($2r$ or $4r$ equal to what?).

畫 sc、bcc、fcc 的慣用立方。每個寫：$N$（每胞原子數）、配位數 CN、原子沿哪個方向相切（$2r$ 或 $4r$ 等於什麼）。
Leave blank — draw on paper
sc: $N=1$, CN $=6$, contact along the cube edge $2r=a$.
bcc: $N=8\times\tfrac18+1=2$, CN $=8$, contact along the body diagonal $4r=\sqrt3\,a$.
fcc: $N=8\times\tfrac18+6\times\tfrac12=4$, CN $=12$, contact along the face diagonal $4r=\sqrt2\,a$.
Corner atom counts as $\tfrac18$, face-centre $\tfrac12$, body-centre $1$.
角 $\tfrac18$、面心 $\tfrac12$、體心 $1$。bcc 沿體對角；fcc 沿面對角。

A2 · derive Three packing fractions (derivation required)

Treat atoms as hard spheres in contact. Write the definition of the packing fraction $\eta$, then derive $\eta$ for sc, bcc, and fcc (include $r$ in terms of $a$). Give the decimal values last. Do not quote the decimals only.

原子當硬球、彼此相切。寫出 $\eta$ 定義，推出 sc、bcc、fcc 的 $\eta$（含 $r$ 與 $a$）。最後才給小數。不准只背 0.52／0.68／0.74。
Derive on paper
$\eta=N\cdot\frac43\pi r^3/a^3$.
sc: $r=a/2$ → $\eta=\pi/6\approx0.524$.
bcc: $r=\sqrt3\,a/4$ → $\eta=\pi\sqrt3/8\approx0.680$.
fcc: $r=\sqrt2\,a/4$ → $\eta=\pi/(3\sqrt2)=\pi\sqrt2/6\approx0.740$. $\eta_{\mathrm{sc}}=\pi/6,\quad\eta_{\mathrm{bcc}}=\pi\sqrt3/8,\quad\eta_{\mathrm{fcc}}=\pi/(3\sqrt2)$

A3 · Miller Intercepts and $d_{hkl}$

(a) A plane intercepts the axes at $4a_1$, $1a_2$, $2a_3$. What are its Miller indices?
(b) What is the difference between the plane $(110)$ and the direction $[110]$? In a cubic crystal, are they perpendicular?
(c) Cubic lattice, $a=3.61$ Å. Find $d_{200}$ and $d_{111}$.

(a) 截距 $4,1,2$ 的 Miller 指標？(b) 面 $(110)$ 與方向 $[110]$ 差在哪？立方晶裡垂直嗎？(c) $a=3.61$ Å，求 $d_{200}$、$d_{111}$。
Write on paper
(a) Reciprocals $\tfrac14,1,\tfrac12$ → multiply by 4 → $(142)$.
(b) $(hkl)$ labels a plane, $[uvw]$ a direction. In the cubic system $[hkl]\perp(hkl)$, so yes $[110]\perp(110)$. Not true in a general crystal system.
(c) $d_{hkl}=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$. $d_{200}=a/2=1.805$ Å; $d_{111}=a/\sqrt3\approx2.08$ Å.
手順：截距 → 取倒數 → 最小整數。立方才保證 $[hkl]\perp(hkl)$。

A4 · trap Is CsCl bcc?

CsCl: Cs at the cube corners, Cl at the body centre (or swapped).
(a) What is the Bravais lattice? What is the basis?
(b) Why is it not bcc?
(c) How many Cs and Cl per conventional cube?

CsCl：Cs 在角、Cl 在體心。(a) Bravais 是哪種？basis 是什麼？(b) 為什麼不是 bcc？(c) 每胞幾顆 Cs、Cl？
Write on paper
(a) Bravais lattice = simple cubic (sc); basis = one Cs + one Cl.
(b) bcc requires every lattice point (corner and centre) to be equivalent — same species, same environment. Here corner and centre are different atoms, so it is sc with a two-atom basis.
(c) Cs: $8\times\tfrac18=1$; Cl: body centre $=1$. Ratio $1:1$.
角與體心不同種 → 不是 bcc。看起來像 bcc，骨子裡是 sc + 兩原子 basis。

A5 · numbers Copper, fcc

Cu is fcc with $a=3.61$ Å. Find (a) the nearest-neighbour distance $d_{\mathrm{nn}}$; (b) $d_{200}$; (c) the hard-sphere radius $r$. Include units.

Cu 為 fcc，$a=3.61$ Å。求 (a) 最近鄰 $d_{\mathrm{nn}}$；(b) $d_{200}$；(c) 硬球半徑 $r$。寫單位。
Compute on paper
(a) $d_{\mathrm{nn}}=a/\sqrt2=3.61/1.414\approx2.55$ Å.
(b) $d_{200}=a/2=1.805$ Å.
(c) $2r=d_{\mathrm{nn}}$ → $r\approx1.28$ Å (equivalently $r=\sqrt2\,a/4$). $d_{\mathrm{nn}}\approx2.55$ Å, $d_{200}=1.805$ Å, $r\approx1.28$ Å

A6 · geometry Ideal hcp $c/a$

Hard-sphere close packing with $2r=a$. Prove that the ideal hcp axial ratio is $c/a=\sqrt{8/3}\approx1.633$. Then: do hcp and fcc have the same packing fraction and CN? How do their stacking sequences differ?

硬球密堆，$2r=a$。證明理想 hcp 的 $c/a=\sqrt{8/3}$。hcp 與 fcc 的填充率、配位數是否相同？堆疊序差在哪？提示：正四面體。
Prove on paper. Hint: regular tetrahedron.
Three mutually touching spheres in the base (edge $a$); the next sphere sits in the hollow → four centres form a regular tetrahedron of edge $a$. Circumradius of the base triangle $R=a/\sqrt3$. Height $h=\sqrt{a^2-R^2}=a\sqrt{2/3}$. The $c$ axis spans two layers: $c=2h=a\sqrt{8/3}$.
$\eta$ and CN: both hcp and fcc have $0.74$ and CN $=12$. Stacking: $ABAB\cdots$ (hcp) vs $ABCABC\cdots$ (fcc). $c/a=\sqrt{8/3}\approx1.633$

Set B · extra · 10–15 min each

B1 Lattice constant from density

Al is fcc, atomic mass $M=27.0\,\mathrm{g\,mol^{-1}}$, density $\rho=2.70\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$. Find $a$ in Å. Take $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$.

Al 為 fcc，$M=27.0$，$\rho=2.70$。求 $a$（Å）。
Compute on paper
$\rho=4M/(N_A a^3)$ → $a=(4M/(N_A\rho))^{1/3}$.
$4M=108$, $N_A\rho\approx1.625\times10^{24}$, $a^3\approx6.65\times10^{-23}$, $a\approx4.05\times10^{-8}\,\mathrm{cm}=4.05$ Å.

B2 Primitive vectors of fcc (disguised)

Take $\mathbf a_1=\frac a2(\hat x+\hat y),\ \mathbf a_2=\frac a2(\hat y+\hat z),\ \mathbf a_3=\frac a2(\hat z+\hat x)$.
Find $|\mathbf a_i|$, the angle between them, and the primitive-cell volume. Which Bravais lattice is this? How many lattice points in the conventional cube?

這組向量是哪種 Bravais？等長嗎、夾角、原胞體積？慣用立方含幾個格點？
Compute on paper
$|\mathbf a_i|=\frac a2\sqrt2$. $\mathbf a_1\cdot\mathbf a_2=a^2/4$ → $\cos\theta=\frac12$, $\theta=60^\circ$.
$V=|\mathbf a_1\cdot(\mathbf a_2\times\mathbf a_3)|=a^3/4$. Four points per conventional cube → fcc.

B3 · quals style Is diamond hard because it packs densely?

Rank the packing fractions of sc, bcc, fcc, and diamond. What is the CN of diamond? Is the statement “diamond is hard because its packing fraction is highest” true? Why?

比較四種填充率。鑽石 CN？「鑽石很硬是因為 packing 最高」對不對？
Write on paper
$\eta$: fcc/hcp $0.74$ $>$ bcc $0.68$ $>$ sc $0.52$ $>$ diamond $\approx0.34$.
Diamond CN $=4$. Hardness comes from directional covalent ($sp^3$ tetrahedral) bonds, not from dense packing — diamond is the emptiest of the four. (2020 quals spirit.)
鑽石最鬆。硬度來自共價鍵，不是填滿。

B4 Lattice $\times$ basis: NaCl and diamond

For each: name the Bravais lattice and the basis. How many Na and Cl in the NaCl conventional cube? How many atoms in the diamond conventional cube?

各寫 Bravais + basis。NaCl 慣用立方幾顆 Na／Cl？diamond 慣用立方幾顆原子？
Write on paper
NaCl: fcc + basis (Na at $000$, Cl at $\tfrac12 00$). Conventional cube: 4 Na + 4 Cl.
diamond: fcc + basis ($000$ and $\tfrac14\tfrac14\tfrac14$, same species). Conventional cube: 8 atoms.

B5 Tungsten, bcc

W is bcc with $a=3.16$ Å. Find the hard-sphere radius $r$ and the nearest-neighbour distance.

W 為 bcc，$a=3.16$ Å。求 $r$ 與最近鄰距離。
Compute on paper
$4r=\sqrt3\,a$ → $r=\sqrt3\,a/4\approx1.37$ Å.
$d_{\mathrm{nn}}=2r=\sqrt3\,a/2\approx2.74$ Å.

B6 Why no five-fold axes?

In two sentences: which rotational orders are allowed in a periodic lattice? Why can a pentagon not tile the plane? How many Bravais lattices in 2D and in 3D?

允許哪些旋轉重數？五邊形為什麼鋪不滿？2D、3D 各幾種 Bravais？
Write on paper
Allowed orders: $1,2,3,4,6$. Five-fold: a regular pentagon cannot tile the plane, so a 5-fold rotation does not map the lattice onto itself. 5 types in 2D, 14 in 3D.
