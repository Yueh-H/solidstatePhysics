Q1 8 min

Define lattice, basis, and crystal structure. Write the general lattice translation $\mathbf T$.

定義 lattice、basis、crystal structure。寫出平移向量 $\mathbf T$。
English on paper
Lattice: the mathematical set of points generated by integer combinations of three primitive vectors.
Basis: the atom(s) attached to each lattice point.
Crystal structure = lattice $\times$ basis. $\mathbf T=n_1\mathbf a_1+n_2\mathbf a_2+n_3\mathbf a_3,\ n_i\in\mathbb Z$

Q2 8 min

For the fcc conventional cube: $N$, coordination number, contact condition ($4r=\ldots$), and packing fraction $\eta$ (derive, do not only quote 0.74).

fcc 慣用立方：$N$、CN、相切條件、推出 $\eta$。
English on paper
$N=8\times\tfrac18+6\times\tfrac12=4$. CN $=12$. Contact along the face diagonal: $4r=\sqrt2\,a$, so $r=\sqrt2\,a/4$. $$\eta=\frac{4\cdot\frac43\pi r^3}{a^3}=\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt2}\approx0.740$$

Q3 8 min

Same as Q2, but for bcc.

bcc：$N$、CN、相切、$\\eta$。
English on paper
$N=2$. CN $=8$. Body diagonal: $4r=\sqrt3\,a$. $$\eta=\frac{2\cdot\frac43\pi r^3}{a^3}=\frac{\pi\sqrt3}{8}\approx0.680$$

Q4 8 min

A plane intercepts the crystal axes at $3a_1$, $2a_2$, $2a_3$. Find the Miller indices. State the cubic formula for $d_{hkl}$ and compute $d_{110}$ if $a=4.00$ Å.

截距 3,2,2 → Miller。立方 $d_{hkl}$。$a=4.00$ Å 的 $d_{110}$。
English on paper
Reciprocals $\frac13,\frac12,\frac12$. Multiply by 6 → $(233)$.
$d_{hkl}=a/\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}$. $d_{110}=4/\sqrt2=2\sqrt2\approx2.83$ Å.
截距 3,2,2 倒數是 1/3, 1/2, 1/2，乘 6 得 (233)。不是 (322)。

Q5 8 min

CsCl has Cs at the cube corners and Cl at the body centre. What is the Bravais lattice? What is the basis? Why is this not bcc?

CsCl 的 Bravais、basis、為什麼不是 bcc。
English on paper
Bravais lattice: simple cubic. Basis: one Cs + one Cl.
bcc requires equivalent lattice points (same species, same environment) at corner and centre. Here they are different atoms, so the lattice is sc with a two-point basis.

Q6 8 min

Copper is fcc, $a=3.61$ Å. Find the nearest-neighbour distance and $d_{200}$. One line of working each.

Cu fcc $a=3.61$ Å：$d_{\mathrm{nn}}$ 與 $d_{200}$。
English on paper
$d_{\mathrm{nn}}=a/\sqrt2\approx2.55$ Å. $d_{200}=a/2=1.805$ Å.

Q7 8 min

Why are only 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 6-fold rotation axes allowed in a Bravais lattice? How many Bravais lattices in 3D?

為什麼沒有五重軸？3D 幾種 Bravais？
English on paper
A rotation must map the lattice onto itself; only $n=1,2,3,4,6$ tile the plane by translations. A 5-fold (pentagon) cannot. There are 14 Bravais lattices in 3D.

Q8 8 min

NaCl: name the Bravais lattice and the basis. How many Na$^+$ and Cl$^-$ in the conventional cube?

NaCl：Bravais、basis、慣用立方幾顆 Na／Cl。
English on paper
Bravais: fcc. Basis: Na at $000$, Cl at $\tfrac12 00$ (two-ion basis).
Conventional cube: 4 Na + 4 Cl.
